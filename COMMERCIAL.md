# G-Code commercial license

Companies, government bodies and other organizations need one seat for each person who uses G-Code in work for the organization. Individuals (including paid and client work, and one-person companies), students, teachers, schools and non-profit charities use G-Code free. [LICENSE](LICENSE) sections 2 and 3 say exactly who pays.

## Price

- **US$39.99 per seat, paid once.** No subscription.
- A seat never expires and covers every version of G-Code that O-Research publishes, including later ones.
- You get updates by pulling them from this GitHub repository. A seat includes no update service, support or warranty.
- A later price change applies only to new purchases. Seats already bought stay as they are.

## What a seat covers

- One named person, on any number of devices they use.
- Everything [LICENSE](LICENSE) allows, used in work for your organization. The limits in section 4 still apply: no reselling or hosting G-Code for a fee, and no business built on G-Code itself, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
- Seats belong to the organization that bought them. When a person stops using G-Code, the organization may move their seat to someone else.

## Trial

An organization may try G-Code for 30 days before buying seats.

## How to buy

Open an issue in this repository titled "Commercial license", with your organization's name and the number of seats.
